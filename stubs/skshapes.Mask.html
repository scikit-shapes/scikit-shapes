skshapes.Mask

class skshapes.Mask(*, indices=None, flat_indices=None, shape, device=None)

Bases: GridStructure

A D-dimensional mask over a voxel grid.

A Mask object can be created from torch.Tensors indicating indices of the voxels contained in the mask. The shape of the mask must also be provided.

Masks are manipulated like sets of voxels. They are also used to select only a subset of the values of an image.

Parameters:

  • indices (Int64[Tensor, '*_'] | None) – The indices of the voxels that are included in the mask. It should be a tensor of shape (N,D) where N is the number of voxels present in the mask.

  • shape (tuple[int, ...]) – The shape of the original grid on which the mask is defined.

  • device (str | device | None) – The device on which the shape is stored (e.g. "cpu" or "cuda"). If None it is inferred from the input.
